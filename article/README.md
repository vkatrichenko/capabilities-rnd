# article/

Phase 3 output — the final deliverable: a short scientific article on AI SDLC security, for
publication on the Provectus website.

Shape, per the charter: evidence and artifacts, not a marketing page. Every claim traces to
something in `artifacts/`, and every conclusion to something in `research/findings/`.

Generalization is the point of this phase. HOPS is the test project, not the subject — a finding
that only holds for a Kotlin/React monorepo with this specific toolchain is not yet a capability.
State what transfers and what does not.

The methodology in `methodology/log.md` is a candidate section: how the research was conducted is
part of what makes the result credible, and Rodion wants the process reusable for future
capabilities.
